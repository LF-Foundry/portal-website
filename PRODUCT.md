# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who regularly use multiple AI services and want quicker desktop access without accumulating browser tabs.

## Product Purpose

Portal gives people one-click access to their AI chats through a floating desktop widget. It succeeds when users can move between the AI services they already use with less browser clutter and less interruption to their work.

## Positioning

Portal presents the official AI websites together inside one floating desktop widget, preserving each service's direct login and native web experience instead of replacing them with Portal's own unified AI API or chat interface.

## Operating Context

Portal is used alongside a person's normal desktop work. Users open services such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini from the widget, sign in through the services' official websites, and switch between them without maintaining a collection of browser tabs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Portal is available for Windows and macOS.
- The website provides product information, downloads, and installation help.
- Release download URLs are centralized in `portal-website/src/lib/downloads.ts` and point to the latest GitHub release assets.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Portal.
- The existing Portal logo is at `portal-website/public/portal-logo.png`.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains the current Portal logo and Windows and macOS installation screenshots under `portal-website/public/`.
- The Help content documents the Windows SmartScreen and macOS security-warning installation flows.
- No testimonials, customer logos, benchmarks, case studies, or press claims are currently present and future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Keep access to frequently used AI services quick and unobtrusive.
- Preserve each AI service's official website, account flow, and native experience.
- Reduce browser-tab clutter while making movement between multiple AI services simple.
